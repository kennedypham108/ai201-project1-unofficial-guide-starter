Title: Dining Dollars FAQ
Source: https://hdhdining.ucsd.edu/dining-plans/dining-dollars-faq.html
Source Type: official UCSD information

Dining Dollars can be used at HDH Dining locations, including residential dining halls and campus markets. They are intended for food purchases within the HDH Dining system. Triton Cash is different because it can be used at HDH Dining locations and at additional participating locations on and off campus.
