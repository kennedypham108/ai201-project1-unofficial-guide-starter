Title: Triton Cash Locations and Flexibility
Source: https://hdhdining.ucsd.edu/dining-plans/index.html
Source Type: official UCSD information

Triton Cash can be used at HDH Dining locations and at more than 60 participating on- and off-campus locations. This makes Triton Cash more flexible than Dining Dollars. It can help students access food and other eligible purchases outside the residential dining system.
