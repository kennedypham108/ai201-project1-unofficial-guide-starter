Title: Budgeting Dining Dollars and Triton Cash
Source: https://hdhdining.ucsd.edu/dining-plans/budgeting.html
Source Type: official UCSD information

UCSD recommends budgeting Dining Dollars across the academic year instead of spending too much early in the quarter. Students should save some Triton Cash for breaks and holidays because some dining locations may close or operate with limited hours. Triton Cash gives students more flexibility during those periods.
