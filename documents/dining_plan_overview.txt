Title: UCSD Dining Plan Overview
Source: https://hdhdining.ucsd.edu/dining-plans/index.html
Source Type: official UCSD information

UCSD residential dining plans use an à la carte system rather than an unlimited all-you-care-to-eat format. Students receive Dining Dollars and choose how to spend them throughout the year. The plan is designed to give students flexibility across HDH Dining locations.
