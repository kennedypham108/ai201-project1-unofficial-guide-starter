Title: Healthy and Affordable Food Options
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/UCSD/comments/1foyncf/any_healthy_cheap_options_on_campus/
Source Type: student opinion

Students recommended comparing portion sizes and prices before choosing a meal. Some suggested buying basic ingredients from campus markets and preparing simple meals instead of purchasing a full prepared meal every time. The discussion emphasized balancing cost, convenience, and nutrition.
