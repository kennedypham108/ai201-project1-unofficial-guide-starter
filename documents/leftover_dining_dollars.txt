Title: Leftover Dining Dollars Recommendations
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/UCSD/comments/1o7u0nz/leftover_dining_dollars_to_spend_where_should_i/
Source Type: student opinion

Students suggested using leftover Dining Dollars at Foodworx for deep-dish pizza. Other suggestions included Makai, Rooftop, Goody's, and campus markets. Some students recommended buying groceries or shelf-stable items from the markets when trying to use a remaining balance.
